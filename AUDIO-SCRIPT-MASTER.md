## HOW TO READ THIS SCRIPT

**Pause markers:**
- `[P]` = short pause — 0.5 seconds. After a punch line. Let it land.
- `[PP]` = medium pause — 1.0 second. Scene breaths. Gear shifts.
- `[PPP]` = long pause — 2.0 seconds. Act breaks. Scene endings.
- `[BREATH]` = natural breath within a long sentence. Don't merge the clauses.
- `[SLOW]` = drop pace here. Let each word arrive separately.
- `[BEAT]` = dramatic hold — no sound. 1 full second of silence.

**Tone markers:**
- `[NOIR]` = low, dry, observational. The detective voice.
- `[INTIMATE]` = lean in. Like telling someone something they already know.
- `[CLINICAL]` = even, measured. The doctor's register.
- `[WARM]` = softer. The father scenes.

**Phonetic notes `{like this}`:**
- Use the phonetic spelling in `{}` whenever marked.
