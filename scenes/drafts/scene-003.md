# Scene 003: The Garbage Man
**Status:** Draft
**Themes:** routine, invisibility, contentment, the unnamed life, universality
**Connects to:** Scene 001, Scene 002

---

## Monologue

The road stretches longer today.

No reason. Same miles. Same turns. Just one of those evenings where the distance between here and home feels like it's being measured in something other than miles.

He appears at the edge of a driveway.

Ordinary man. Ordinary task. Dragging the bin to the curb the way it's been dragged a thousand times before — the wheels catching on the same crack in the pavement, the lid rattling the same rattle. He sets it down. Turns back toward the house.

Doesn't look up.

The detective slows without meaning to.

Was he angry that nobody else did it? Did he volunteer, quiet and uncelebrated, the way good people do? Did he even think about it at all — or did his body just know, the way bodies know, and carry him out there on autopilot while his mind was somewhere else entirely?

Nobody will ever know.

But here's what happened whether he noticed or not.

He breathed fresh air. Cool evening, hint of cut grass and something distant burning. His lungs filled. His blood moved. His heart — that relentless, unglamorous machine — pumped without being asked. His feet found the pavement. His hands did the thing.

Happy or not.

The body doesn't wait for the verdict.

It just keeps going.

The car passes. The man disappears in the mirror. The lead character of his own film, standing at the end of his driveway as the credits roll on another ordinary scene that nobody will remember.

Except the detective remembered.
