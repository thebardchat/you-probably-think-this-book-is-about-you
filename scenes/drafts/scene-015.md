# Scene 015: Left Behind
**Status:** Draft
**Themes:** memory, loss, the irretrievable, the mundane, anxiety, the dream that lingers
**Connects to:** Scene 013

---

## Monologue

Something is missing.

Not gone. Missing. There is a difference and the body knows it before the brain catches up — that specific hollow inventory that runs itself without permission somewhere between the driveway and the point of no return.

Did you leave it.

The glasses. The world going soft at the edges, the screen a suggestion, words approximated. You squint like that helps. It doesn't help. You know it doesn't help. You squint anyway because the alternative is admitting the glasses are on the kitchen counter next to the coffee you also forgot.

The wallet. Fourteen dollars on a card that lives in a pocket you are now not certain you're wearing. The old man at the counter waiting. The cashier waiting. Everyone waiting on the small rectangle that makes you a legitimate participant in the transaction of the day.

The hat. Smaller loss. Larger irritation. The hat was part of the plan.

The shoes. You'd have noticed by now.

The pants.

Everyone has the dream. You know the one. Standing somewhere that requires pants and discovering the oversight simultaneously with everyone else in the vicinity. Waking damp and grateful. The relief of the sheets. The ceiling. The blessed ordinariness of a room that contains your pants.

But what about the things with no shape.

The things you can't pat your pockets for. The thing you said to someone in 2009 that landed wrong and never got corrected. The version of yourself you were in a particular year that you cannot locate now no matter which drawer you check. The apology that almost happened. The phone call that kept getting scheduled for later until later became a different kind of permanent.

Can you go back for those.

Is it there if you go back.

The detective pats his pockets.

Keys. Phone. Wallet.

Everything accounted for.

He drives.

The other things ride along in the passenger seat saying nothing, taking up no space, costing nothing.

Just there.

Just missing.
