# Scene 012: The Billion
**Status:** Draft
**Themes:** dreams, the subconscious, chaos, pattern, the familiar made strange
**Connects to:** Scene 011

---

## Monologue

The ceiling becomes the floor becomes a highway becomes the gas station but the gas station is underwater and the old man is there standing too close to a fish that owes him money and the fish knows it and the fish is not apologetic.

The truck drives itself.

It has always driven itself.

The stars come down to road level and they are not stars they are screens and every screen is playing the same movie and the lead character keeps changing faces but the eyes stay the same — yours, always yours, looking back from every angle like a funhouse that forgot to be funny.

A billion things move in formation.

Each one small. Each one certain. Together they make a shape that almost means something — bird, or wave, or the answer to a question he forgot asking. They reconfigure. New shape. Almost. Not quite. The meaning stays one frame ahead like a word on the tip of the tongue of the universe.

The billboard is there.

*We Will Treat You Like Family.*

Behind it, the family. All of them. The small string one still grinning. The wife lit warm. The sons at various heights and distances, arranged the way they are in the part of the brain that never stops keeping track. They wave. He tries to wave back. His arm is cruise control. His arm has decided its own speed.

A door rattles.

Cupholder. Something shifting. Not enough to matter. Just enough to remind.

The detective is at his own desk reading his own file and the file is blank except for one line repeated down every page in increasingly small handwriting until it becomes texture, becomes pattern, becomes the grain of the wood of the desk itself:

*You probably think this is about you.*

The garbage man appears at the edge of the dream dragging something that is not garbage. He does not look up. He never looks up. The detective has decided this is either tragedy or enlightenment and cannot find the border between them.

Somewhere a hammer drops.

A billion things scatter.

Regroup.

Almost a shape again.

Almost.
