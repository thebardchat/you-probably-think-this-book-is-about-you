# Scene 011: The Rebuttal
**Status:** Draft
**Themes:** fatherhood, chaos, love, the body, aftermath, joy
**Connects to:** Scene 010

---

## Monologue

Victory, it turns out, has a follow up.

The champion — unbothered by mercy, unconcerned with diplomacy — launches the second phase of the campaign before the treaty is signed. No warning. No declaration. Just small determined hands finding purchase on the chest of a grown man and executing a maneuver that has no name in any martial art but has existed in the arsenal of small children since the beginning of recorded suffering.

The detective does not cry out.

He considers it.

The little string one grins with the full face. All teeth. Eyes lit like something feral and beloved. This is not cruelty. This is intimacy — the specific language of a child who trusts you enough to be completely unreasonable with your body. Only the truly loved get handled this way.

He knows this.

It doesn't help.

The detective laughs.

Somewhere between the arm wrestling and the rebuttal and the laugh he couldn't stop — the day dissolved. The billboard receded. The stars stopped being countable and became just stars. The rattle in the cupholder went quiet in his memory.

All of it.

For this.

Ten sixteen PM and a small person is dismantling him and he would not move an inch.
