# Scene 001: The Lead Character
**Status:** Draft
**Themes:** ego, perception, identity, self-deception, universality
**Connects to:** TBD

---

## Monologue

It's your favorite movie, and you're the lead character.

You always have been.

The camera follows you — has always followed you — through every dimly lit hallway, every kitchen argument, every moment you sat alone in a car in a parking lot wondering how the plot got this complicated. You didn't choose the role. Nobody does. But somewhere between the opening credits and the part where things got heavy, you decided you were the one the story was about.

And here's the thing about that.

You're right.

It *is* your story. Every face you've ever seen was a supporting character moving through your frame. Every conversation was a scene written for your development. Every loss was a plot device. Every miracle was foreshadowing. The whole thing — the mess, the beauty, the unbearable Tuesday afternoons — all of it was building toward *you*.

But here's what they don't tell you at the beginning.

Every single other person on this planet is the lead character of the exact same movie. Seven billion directors. Seven billion cameras. Seven billion stories that are each the most important story ever told.

And all of them — *all of them* — think yours is a supporting role.

So who's right?

The detective leans back in the chair, lets the smoke curl toward the water-stained ceiling, and considers the case.

Everybody. Nobody. Same thing.

The film keeps rolling either way.
