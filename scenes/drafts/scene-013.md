# Scene 013: Still Asleep?
**Status:** Draft
**Themes:** waking, the seam, reality's bad impression of itself
**Connects to:** Scene 012

---

## Monologue

The cupholder is rattling.

He knows this sound.

The road is familiar in the way that things are familiar before you check whether they should be. The stars are where stars go during the day which is nowhere visible which is fine. The truck moves at a speed that feels decided.

Everything is normal.

Everything is exactly normal.

He does not check.

You didn't either.
