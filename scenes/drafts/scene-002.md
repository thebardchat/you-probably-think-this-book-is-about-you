# Scene 002: Tuned In, Tuned Out
**Status:** Draft
**Themes:** disconnection, distraction, irony, modern ego, invisibility
**Connects to:** Scene 001

---

## Monologue

The sun is going down.

Nobody notices.

Not the way it deserves — that slow bleed of orange across the horizon, the kind of thing painters used to die trying to capture. It happens every single evening like a standing ovation for the day, and the audience is staring at their laps.

Every passing car a small dark theater. Every face lit blue-white from the glow of a four-inch screen. The lead character of their favorite movie, scrolling through someone else's.

In a hurry to get somewhere. Somewhere important. Somewhere that will feel exactly like the last somewhere once they arrive.

The detective watches from the slow lane. Notes the irony. They are connected to everything and present for nothing. Tuned in to a frequency that carries every voice except the one worth hearing — the one that says *look up, the sky is on fire and it's doing it for free.*

But who is he to talk.

He's driving too.

The sun finishes its exit without applause.

The road keeps going.
