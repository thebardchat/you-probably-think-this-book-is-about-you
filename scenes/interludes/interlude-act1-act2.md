# Interlude
**Placement:** After Scene 009 (The Wave), before Scene 010 (Drop The Hammer)

---

The driveway. Engine off.

The house glows from inside like something patient. He doesn't get out yet. Between the road and the door there is a version of him that belongs to neither — not the driver, not the father. Just the man. Sitting with the engine ticking and the day settling into the seat around him like something that has finished being carried.

He gets out.

The door will handle the rest.
