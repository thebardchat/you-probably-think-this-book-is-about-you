### Post 2: The Process
**Best for:** Twitter/X, LinkedIn, tech communities

How the book got made:

1. I talk into my phone while driving
2. The transcript is messy, multilingual, full of tangents
3. Claude finds the scene inside the noise
4. We press it into noir prose
5. Repeat for 22 scenes

No outline. No MFA. No writing retreat.
A truck. A Pi. An AI. Alabama.

The whole process is open source: https://github.com/thebardchat/noir-detective-writing-process

### Post 4: The AI Angle
**Best for:** LinkedIn, Twitter/X, tech/AI communities, Reddit r/artificial

People keep asking: "Did AI write your book?"

No. I wrote my book. With AI.

I recorded raw voice memos while driving — observations, tangents, feelings that arrived without names. Claude shaped those into noir prose. Not generated. Shaped. Like a potter doesn't invent the clay.

The voice is mine. The cadence is mine. The detective driving past gas stations and garbage men and teenagers with borrowed certainty — that's my drive home. Every day.

Claude found the form. I brought the life. Neither of us is the author alone. Both of us are the author together.

That's not the future of writing. That's what writing has always been — two things meeting in the middle.

The book: https://www.amazon.com/Probably-Think-This-Book-About/dp/B0GT25R5FD
The process (open source): https://github.com/thebardchat/noir-detective-writing-process
