About the Book

What It Is

A collection of noir-style vignettes about ego, identity, and the universal human condition. Written in third person. Dark but light. Cynical but comical.

The Central Theme

A detective who discovers he is the case. 22 scenes that hold up a mirror not just to one character, but to every reader who has ever been the hero of their own story.

The Voice

Tight. Dry. Noir. Written under the ShaneBrain Constitution — every word shaped by real experience in Hazel Green, Alabama.

Format

Available as Kindle ebook, audiobook, and print on Amazon. Complete first draft. 22 scenes, fully written.
