# You Probably Think This Book Is About You

Governed by the [TheShaneBrain Constitution](https://github.com/thebardchat/constitution).

## What This Is

A collection of noir-style vignettes about ego, identity, and the universal human condition. Written in third person. Dark but light. Cynical but comical. Complete first draft — 22 scenes.

## Reading Order

Scenes read 001 → 013, 015 → 025, then 014. Scene 014 is the hidden finale — it was intentionally skipped in sequence and placed at the end of the book. The reader carries the gap without knowing, then finds it as the last page.

## Sacred Rules — Do Not Break These

1. **Scene 012 (The Billion) has NO notes.** This is intentional. The dream does not explain itself. Scene 013's notes retroactively explain both scenes — that's the trick.
2. **Scene 014 is the FINAL scene.** It lives after 025. It is not "missing" — it is the ending hidden in plain sight. Never reorder it into the middle.
3. **Scene 018 (Dr. Seen) is the SPINE.** Every scene before it builds toward it. Every scene after it answers to it. Status: SPINE, Connects To: ALL.
4. **No scene 014 exists in sequence.** The manifest jumps 013 → 015. This is structural, not an error.
5. **The detective is never named.** Neither is anyone else. "The small string one" is the youngest son. The reader doesn't need names — they know these people.

## Recurring Characters

- **The detective** — the observer, the driver, the lead character who discovers he's the case
- **The old man** — stands too close at the gas station, put the question down in 1987
- **The garbage man** — doesn't look up, the body votes, present not happy
- **The teenagers** — blank pages, borrowed certainty, one becomes the detective
- **The driver** — anonymous, the wave that may or may not have happened
- **The small string one** — almost five, drops the hammer, opens the door at the end
- **The doctor** — no instruments, no diagnosis, reads the files, the brain was never broken
- **The dog** — the veteran, surveys the returning figure, claims his post

ACT III — The Inventory (015-017)
  What's left behind. The things with no shape. The long run.

SPINE — Dr. Seen (018)
  The examined brain. Everything connects here.

ACT IV — The Aftermath (019-020)
  Not a bill. Same road, different hands.

ACT V — The Files (021-024)
  The background characters get their files. Each one ahead of the detective.

  Full circle. The world unchanged. The observer shifted.

FINALE — You Finally Found (014)
  The missing scene. The ending hidden in plain sight. The last page.
```

docs/MANIFEST.md   — scene index with status, themes, connections

```

## Scene File Format

Each scene has: title, status, themes, connections, monologue, and notes (except 012).
Status values: Draft, SPINE (018 only), FINAL (014 only), Locked, Archived.

## Voice & Tone

- Noir third person. The detective narrates but is not above it — he's in it too.
- Short punches followed by long runs. Cadence matters.
- No lectures. No sermons. Witness, don't judge.
- Humor is dry, placed where the reader doesn't expect it.
- Callbacks land as echoes, not repetition. Same image, different weight.
